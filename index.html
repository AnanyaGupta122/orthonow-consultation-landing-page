Is Joint Pain Slowing You Down?

  • Desk Worker's Back Pain

    Lower back stiffness and pain from prolonged sitting at your workstation.

  • Weekend Warrior Knee Pain

    Sharp or aching knee pain during or after running, cycling, or playing sports.

  • Chronic Joint Ache

    Persistent discomfort that affects your daily mobility and sleep quality.

When Should You See a Specialist?

  • ⚠ Pain lasting longer than 48 hours without improvement.
  • ⚠ Inability to put weight on your knee or stand comfortably.
  • ⚠ Numbness or tingling radiating down your legs.
  • ⚠ Swelling, redness, or warmth around the joint.
